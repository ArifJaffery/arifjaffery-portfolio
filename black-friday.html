In your happiness, remember others

Black Friday is a lot of fun, but it isn't a happy season for everyone. Some people around us have lost their job this year, some are choosing between bills and groceries, and some will spend Christmas on their own. As you fill your cart, leave a little room for someone who needs it more. A small kindness can make someone's whole season.

Shop it like a pro

Brazilian shoppers nicknamed early sales "Black Fraude" over prices quietly raised before the discount. A few habits keep you on the right side of it.

Know the real price

Look up what the item sold for in October before trusting a "was" price. Price-history tools and saved screenshots help.

Compare the model number

Sale-only variants of vacuums and air fryers can differ slightly. Match the exact model across retailers.

Price-match it

Big electronics chains often match a competitor's in-stock price. Bring the link to the counter or chat.

Count the install

A split-system's price rarely includes installation. Get the full installed quote before you compare.
