The voice

India's most versatile playback singer

Sonu Nigam is one of India's most celebrated playback singers. His voice moves between romantic melodies, emotional ballads, classical pieces and high-energy numbers with the same ease, in Hindi and many other Indian languages.

He began singing as a child alongside his father, Agam Kumar Nigam. He rose to prominence in the 1990s and became one of Bollywood's most loved voices, with multiple Filmfare Awards and National Film Award recognition along the way.
